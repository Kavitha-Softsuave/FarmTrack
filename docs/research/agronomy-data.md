### 1c. "Points for consideration" (V; these become rules and advisory notes)
1. The schedule is for normal weather.
2. **Heavy rain within 12 h of spraying → repeat the spray within 7 days.** "Heavy" is not defined in mm (NF), so make it a config value (default 10 mm).
3. **Do not repeat the same chemical.**
4. Spray only when there is a chance of disease.
5. **Dodine:** do not mix with other products; do not spray above 30 °C or in slow-drying conditions; do not use hard water.
6. Avoid captan for 15 days before and after an oil spray.
7. Do not tank-mix micronutrients, PGRs or hormones (russeting risk).
8. Leaf litter: compost it, or spray 5% urea on the orchard floor.
9. In scab-prone areas, keep a 12–14 day interval up to the primary scab stage.
10. **White root rot:** carbendazim 0.1% drench, or aureofungin 0.05% + CuSO₄ 0.05%, at the onset of rains.
11. **Collar rot:** Bordeaux paint in winter; mancozeb 0.3% drench.
12. **Hail:** immediately carbendazim 100 g or mancozeb 600 g per 200 L; within 3–4 days boric acid 200 g + ZnSO₄ 500 g + quicklime 250 g; after 10–12 days micronutrients + urea 1 kg.
13. Keep insecticide use minimal in April–June to protect beneficial insects.

**Nauni rainy-season advisory, July 2026 (V):**
- Protectant: metiram 600 g/200 L.
- If severe: Lustre 160 ml, Cabrio Top 200 g, Shamir 500 ml, Luna Experience 126 ml, or Avtaar 500 g.
- Cuprofix Disperss 1200 g within 24 h of harvest.
- Source: https://thenewshimachal.com/2026/07/horticulture-university-issues-advisory-for-apple-disease-management-during-rainy-season/

**SKUAST-Kashmir 2026 (second opinion, per 100 L):**
- Protectants at green tip **only if a full day of rain is forecast; skip if 7 dry days are forecast.**
- Stop sprays 3–4 weeks before harvest.
- Keep 3–4 days between insecticide and fungicide sprays.

## 2. Weather → disease rules
**Apple scab: revised Mills table** (MacHardy & Gadoury 1989, via Penn State; V)
- Hours of continuous leaf wetness needed for infection, by mean temperature during the wet period:

| °C | 1.1 | 2.2 | 2.8 | 3.9 | 5.0 | 6.1 | 7.2 | 7.8 | 8.9 | 10 | 11.1 | 12.2–13.3 | 13.9–15 | 16.1–23.9 | 25 | 26.1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wet h | 41 | 35 | 30 | 28 | 21 | 18 | 15 | 13 | 12 | 11 | 9 | 8 | 7 | 6 | 8 | 11 |

- Join wet periods separated by less than 8 h.
- Lesions appear 9–17 days after infection.
- Infection is rare above about 26 °C.
- The primary-infection season runs from green tip to about 3 weeks after petal fall.

**Marssonina blotch / premature leaf fall (Himachal; V-s, ICAR abstracts)**
- Appears from the 2nd week of June; defoliation in July–August.
- Risk when mean temperature is above 20 °C and RH is about 70% for 5–6 days.
- Infection needs at least 6 h of wetness (4 h at 20–25 °C, 8 h when cooler); about 40 h for an epidemic.
- No validated model for Himachal (NF).

**Alternaria leaf blotch:** monsoon (June–August), same humid >20 °C trigger (V-s).

**Mites:** hot, dry weather favours them; build-up in July.

**Late blight, potato/tomato (NIPHM Potato package, p.17; V; ready to code):**
- Over 7 days, if the hours at 7.2–26.6 °C total at least 115 **and** the hours with RH >85% total at least 90 → late blight is expected within 10 days.

**FRAC limits for pome fruit (V):**
- **DMI:** at most 4 sprays per season.
- **QoI:** only in mixtures; at most 3 per season (4 if there are 12 or more sprays in total).
- **SDHI:** at most 2 consecutive applications; season maximum depends on the total number of sprays.
- **HP rule:** do not repeat the same chemical.

**Rain wash-off:**
- **Official HP rule:** heavy rain within 12 h of spraying → repeat within 7 days.
- **Captan:** about 1 mm of rain removes about 50% of the residue; treat trees as unprotected after about 50 mm (NC State).
- **Rainfastness:** contact products need 8–24 h before rain; systemics 1–2 h; mancozeb at least 4 h (Purdue).
- **Proposed MVP config (I):**
  - protectant sprayed less than 12 h before more than 10 mm of rain → re-spray within 7 days
  - cumulative rain above 25 mm → shorten the spray interval
  - cumulative rain above 50 mm → treat as unprotected
  - systemics are rainfast after 2 h

## 8. Addendum, 19 Oct 2026: re-verification against the rendered HP 2026 PDF
The main session rendered pages 2-5 and 8-11 of the HP 2026 PDF and read them directly.
- **Dose tables confirmed:** all fungicide and insecticide/acaricide doses in section 1 match. Chlorothalonil + difenoconazole has a **blank target cell at petal fall** (and "premature leaf fall" at walnut+20d), as recorded.
- **Missing row added:** the insecticide table has a sixth stage, "Fruit development, 20 days after the above spray at S. No. 5": fenazaquin 10 EC 50 ml, propargite 57 EC 200 ml, cyenopyrafen 30 SC 50 ml (stage id `walnut_plus_40d`).
- **Waiting periods:** all match the "Pesticides formulations recommended and evaluated" table. Printed "-" means none stated.
- **Brand table (new, verified):** 29 fungicide groups and 11 insecticide/acaricide/oil groups, 103 brands, with manufacturer where printed. Stored in `data/reference/brand_master.csv`. The table is "recommended and evaluated" brands, not an exhaustive approval list. Thiacloprid is "21.7% EC" in the dose table and "SC" in the brand table.
- **Point 7 (stronger than recorded in section 1c):** "Do not mix any other pesticide/chemical/micro-nutrient/growth regulator/hormone with the above recommended pesticides to avoid russeting and other disorders. When necessary, these may be sprayed separately."
- **Point 13:** drench the basin with chlorpyriphos after harvest at 4 ml/L "for the control of Woolly Apple Aphid and Root Borer". **Point 14:** minimum insecticide in April-June for ladybird beetles, syrphid flies and other beneficials.
- **Preventive measures (a-e)** and **fruit-size** guidance (GA4+7 1.8% + BA 1.8%, 25-30 ppm, 277-333 ml per 200 L at 80-90% king bloom) are in the advisory notes.
- **Proposed-ban note:** captan, carbendazim, chlorpyriphos, dimethoate, malathion, mancozeb, monocrotophos, thiophanate-methyl, zineb, ziram, "vide Gazette Notification No. CG-DL-E-18052020-219423" (18 May 2020 draft).
- **Safety section:** it prints a Poison Control number, 1-800-222-1222, which looks like a US number. The app must not show it.
- **Stage timing (new):** Priyanka et al. 2021 (IJCMAS 10(1):686-692), 2016-17 Julian-day means for flowering: about 100 at 1500-2000 m, about 114 at 2000-2500 m, about 118 at 2500-3000 m. Bud development about F-38 days and inflorescence emergence about F-18 days. The paper's later "fruit development" and "maturity" columns are not harvest dates and were not used. The calendar built from this is an estimate (`data/reference/stage_calendar.csv`).
